Decimal Frog Hop!

Multiplying & dividing decimals by 10, 100, 1000 and more

🐸 Meet Dot the Frog

Every decimal number has a tiny red decimal point. In this pond, the point rides on the back of a frog named Dot. 🐸

The digits are lily pads — they never move. Only Dot hops! And Dot only follows two rules:
✖️ ➡️
MULTIPLY = hop RIGHTThe number gets BIGGER 🚀
⬅️ ➗
DIVIDE = hop LEFTThe number gets SMALLER 🐜
🔢 How many hops? Count the zeros!  10 → 1 hop  ·  100 → 2 hops  ·  1000 → 3 hops
🪷 Empty lily pad? If Dot lands past the digits, fill the empty pads with 0 so nobody falls in the water!
🕵️ Whole number? Dot is hiding at the very end: 78 is really 78. — so 78 × 100 → hop 2 → 7800

10ⁿ The Little Number Secret

Sometimes your teacher writes 10² or 10³ instead of 100 or 1000. The little number up top is a hop counter! Tap each card to flip it.

⚠️ Trap alert! 10³ is not 10 × 3 = 30. It is 10 × 10 × 10 = 1000. So × 10³ means 3 hops right.

✂️ Partitioning: × 20, × 30, × 70…

Partitioning means breaking a number into friendly parts. ✂️
There's no button for × 70… but 70 is just 7 × 10! So we break it apart, do the 7 part, then let Dot do the × 10 part.
70
7×10

🗺️ The 4-step map

✂️1. Split70 → 7 × 10
🙈2. Hide the point2.68 → 268
268 × 7 = 1876
📍3. Point back2.68 had 2 decimal places → 18.76
🐸4. Hop × 1018.76 → 187.6
🛑 Don't stop at step 3! 2.46 × 2 = 4.92 is only halfway. The 20 still has a × 10 hiding inside it → 4.92 × 10 = 49.2.
🤔 Sense check: 2.46 × 20 is about 2 × 20 = 40. Is 4.92 near 40? No way! Is 49.2? Yes! ✅
